# Dashboard and every API-connected screen

## Scope

Operate mode. The director's home, the shell, sign-in, and every routed screen that reads the backend: access, employees, attendance, leaves, payroll, HR settings, finance, exchange rates, alerts. Arabic default, English available.

## Audience

Company director, HR and finance officers, at a desk in daylight. The user asked for administrative colours with taste and a highly professional finish, saw Emerald and Bronze built, then pinned Slate and Indigo instead: light, modern, one indigo for actions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet slate office where one indigo does the acting and shows where you are. It refuses decoration: no second accent, no shadow theatre, no dark frame.

OWN-WORLD: Slate ground #F2F3F8, white rail and surfaces, ink #14162B, hairlines #E1E3EE. Indigo #4F46C8 for primary buttons, links, the active rail item (on an indigo tint), the active tab and the datum under each page title. Lavender #B9B5F0 as the second data series and chart baseline. Red, amber and green state a condition only. Readex Pro for every word. 12px surfaces, 8px controls.

STORY: The director opens the home, reads the headline figures in one joined strip, sees what waits, and opens a module from the rail. The same heading, strip, filter bar and sheet repeat on every screen, so nothing has to be relearned.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 256px white rail at the inline start: logo, company line, grouped links, the active one an indigo-tint pill, account card at the foot. White top bar with search, language, bell and account. Work column: the title over a hairline with a 72px indigo segment, actions at the far edge, then the joined figure strip, then chart panels.

FORM: User-pinned palette after seeing the first build; a grounded direction of ours, not a dealt challenger. seed:ca9c2db294520c93. Signature move: the datum, one hairline with an indigo segment shared by every page title, tab row and the sign-in title. The sign-in scene is the one place the indigo goes deep.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved

Currency, salary components and logo variants stay as supplied. The 17 unrouted legacy screens are out of scope.
